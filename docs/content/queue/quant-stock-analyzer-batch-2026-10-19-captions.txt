=== 1) STOP LOSS (quant-stock-analyzer-stoploss-en.mp4) ===
Decide your exit BEFORE you buy 🛑 A stop loss sells automatically when the price hits your limit. Buy at $100, stop at $90 = loss capped near 10 percent (gaps can skip it). Risk only 1-2 percent of your account per trade.
💾 Save this before your next trade
📩 Send it to a friend who "just holds and hopes"

=== 2) DIVERSIFICATION (quant-stock-analyzer-diversification-en.mp4) ===
One stock = one single point of failure 🎯 $10,000 in one stock that falls 40 percent loses $4,000. Spread over 10 stocks, the same drop in one costs $400. Diversification cuts single-company risk, not market risk.
💾 Save this
📩 Send it to a friend who is all-in on one stock
Not financial advice. Education only.
#stocks #investing #diversification #etf #portfolio #stockmarket #investingforbeginners #financialeducation

=== 3) DIVIDEND TRAP (quant-stock-analyzer-dividendtrap-en.mp4) ===
A 12 percent dividend yield isn't free money ⚠️ Yield = yearly dividend / share price. When the price crashes, the yield jumps up. Check if profit and cash flow can really pay it.
💾 Save this before you chase income
📩 Send it to a friend who screens stocks by yield
Not financial advice. Education only.
