**HOOK**
I gave a trading bot 10,000 dollars of fake money. After 21 trades, here is what actually happened.

**1 - The first day**
On day one, I opened 6 trades on 6 different tickers. Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, XRP, Nvidia, Apple. All long. All of them got stopped out.
Six names, but really just one single bet. That is not diversification.

**2 - The good day**
Later, one day, five trades hit their take profit. Longs and shorts mixed. Together, about plus 322 dollars.
The rule was always the same: stop at one ATR, target at two ATR. No gut feeling.

**3 - The best and the worst**
My best trade was a silver short I held for 15 days: plus 220 dollars.
My worst was a Google long that hit its stop loss: minus 194 dollars.
Both followed the same rules. One won, one lost. That is trading.

**4 - Today**
Today the account sits at 10,711 dollars, up about 7 percent. It is not real money, but the lessons are real.
And some days the best trade is no trade.
